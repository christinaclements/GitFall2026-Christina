Fourth Wing follows Violet Sorrengail, a young scholar 
forced by her militant mother to enter the Riders Quadrant at 
Basgiath War College, where only those who bond with dragons survive. 
Despite constant danger from brutal training and hostile cadets, Violet’s
 intelligence and resilience help her endure, ultimately forming 
 unprecedented bonds with peers and uncovering a hidden truth about
  a far darker enemy threatening their kingdom.
